# Memory techniques and where they appear

Contents: encoding, organising, recall, state and habits, mapping table.

These come from the learner's own Jim Kwik masterclass and "Super Brain" notes. Apply them in the notes without naming or teaching them.

## Encoding (getting it in)
- Information times emotion equals memory: make images loud, funny, physical. Used in memory palaces and analogies.
- Place, Imagine, Entwine (PIE): pick a familiar place, imagine a vivid image of the concept, entwine them with action and sound. Used for the palace stops.
- Vivid image checklist (Action, Emotion, Illogical, Outstanding, Unusual): rate your own images against it. Used when writing palace stops.
- Acronyms and silly sentences for ordered lists. Used for the hooks box.
- Chain stories: link items in a short story when order matters (for example a pipeline).
- Analogies to what the learner already knows: new idea hooks onto an old one.

## Organising
- Capture and Create: write what the source says, then rewrite it in your own words. Used in the gist table.
- Primacy and recency: people remember the start and end best, so the Top 3 box opens and closes each note.
- Chunk into small steps (5 to 6 boxes in a diagram, 4 to 5 palace stops).

## Recall (getting it out)
- Active recall beats rereading: flip cards, quizzes, blank prompts the learner must answer before seeing the answer.
- Spaced repetition: revisit after 1, 3, 7, 21 days (day tracker) and with Leitner boxes at the card level.
- Teach it: explaining to someone, or a mirror, within a day. Used for the teach box.
- Interleaving: weekly and monthly recaps mix questions from different days.

## State and habits
- FAST: Forget (clear the mind first), Active, State (energy and mood), Teach. Used in the pre-study check and teach box.
- 45 minutes of focus, then a 5-minute brain break with movement (cross-crawl), water, eyes far away. Used in the timer.
- Anchors: the same scent while studying and before an interview. Used as a checkbox in the pre-study check.
- Celebrate micro-victories: XP, streak, small confetti for finishing things.
